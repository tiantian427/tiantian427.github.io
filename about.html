About

Researching institutions through unusual data.

My work asks how formal authority, regulation, and public commitment change behavior when implementation is decentralized, incentives are incomplete, and markets are institutionally constructed.

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Finance at Guanghua School of Management, Peking University.

I work across political economy, financial economics, and public economics. Empirically, I combine large-scale administrative records, regulatory and judicial texts, market data, and computational methods to study institutional design and strategic adaptation.
